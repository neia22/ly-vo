Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 June 2026

Lyvo is operated by Open House Ventures (“Lyvo”, “we”, “us”). This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Lyvo you agree to this policy.

1. Who we are

Open House Ventures, based in Namibia, operates the Lyvo website and mobile app — a platform that connects property owners and renters. You can reach us at hello@ly-vo.app.

2. Information we collect

  • Account details — your name, email address, country, and whether you are a property owner or renter.
  • Renter information — identity documents, proof of income (such as payslips or bank statements), employment and rental history, and the details you include in an application.
  • Property owner information — property details and proof of ownership you provide for verification.
  • Transaction & activity data — applications, tour bookings, leases, messages, and your subscription status.
  • Technical data — device information, app and log data used to operate and secure the service.

3. How we use your information

  • To provide and operate the Lyvo marketplace and your account.
  • To verify identity and income through trusted providers and to help property owners screen applicants.
  • To process applications, viewings, leases and subscriptions, and to communicate with you about them.
  • To keep Lyvo safe — preventing fraud, abuse and forged documents — and to comply with the law.
  • To improve our products and develop new features.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only as follows:

  • With property owners and renters — when you apply for a home, the property’s owner receives your application, including your verification results and proof of income. Property owner and listing details are shown to renters.
  • With service providers who process data on our behalf: Supabase (secure hosting and database), Resend (email), Smile ID (identity verification), Inscribe (income/document analysis), HubSpot (waitlist), and licensed payment partners once payments launch.
  • For legal and safety reasons — to comply with law, enforce our terms, or protect rights and safety.

5. Storage and security

Your documents are stored privately and encrypted, with access controls that limit them to you and the owner of a home you apply to. We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, though no system can be guaranteed perfectly secure.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Access & correction — you can view and update your profile in the app.
  • Deletion — you can permanently delete your account, listings, applications and documents from Profile → Account settings → Delete account, or by emailing us.
  • You may also contact us to ask about the information we hold about you.

7. International processing

Some of our service providers process data on servers outside Namibia. Where they do, we rely on those providers’ security and contractual protections.

8. Children

Lyvo is intended for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version here and update the date above. Significant changes will be communicated in the app or by email.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email hello@ly-vo.app.
